# Topicquay brand system

Position: GCSE revision planner printable for GCSE students and their parents, with calm, practical page design.
Primary #283b65; accent #d4e478; nine-step neutral ramp and status colours in assets/tokens.css. Display: space-grotesk; text: Source Sans 3. SIL OFL fonts and licences included. Scale: mobile 45/34/23/18/15; desktop 76/48/23/21/18/15.

Logo directions considered: pure wordmark (clear but generic); open-path symbol + wordmark (selected); initial monogram (rejected as generic). Mark is an original open book, not a boxed letter. Favicons use the same open-path symbol. No invented affiliations.

Imagery: actual buyer PDF page renders only. assets/preview-1.webp is complete PDF page 1; preview-2.webp is complete PDF page 3; rasterised by pdftoppm, scaled uniformly, no invented UI/data. CSS frames do not alter the asset. No stock-photo or image-model claim. Worked data in PDF/XLSX is labelled fictional.

Voice: British, plain and specific. Do say “one-off”, not “lifetime value”; “printable PDF”, not “powerful system”; “recorded costs”, not “guaranteed profit”; “plan a task”, not “ace your exams”; “planned price / coming soon”, not fake urgency. User-specific coming-soon/no-checkout and privacy wording take precedence over generic landing-page skill bans.

Three headline directions: outcome (Give every revision session a job., selected); format (GCSE revision planner printable); generic benefit (“Make planning simpler”, rejected as vague). Independent Humantestbot/CEOtestbot selection unavailable: self-review only, not a PASS from them.

Shared benchmark patterns: Tide (short outcome headline); Xero (real product visual); Jobber (specific workflow); Checkatrade (clear audience); Monzo Business (price clarity); Tradify (workflow sequence); Fergus (task-led product explanation); Pleo (high-contrast hierarchy). These are design references, not customers, endorsements or verified screenshots. No paid reference libraries used.

Design deviation: no Refero/Mobbin connection or external review agent in this executor. No reference screenshot board or independent acceptance is claimed. Exact user-requested real-product imagery supersedes trade-photography default for these non-trades packs.
